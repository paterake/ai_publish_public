If you direct AI coding agents, you approve options the agent wrote, and it marks one "(Recommended)". It records your yes as your decision, and the next agent treats it as your intent.

I call this Coding Agent Recommendation Bias and Fatigue:

- You approve a recommendation that later has to be reverted, and the same kind of approval comes round again.
- You approve on the agent's framing, without the facts to decide.
- The asks keep coming, so you pick the recommended option to keep the work moving.

I know of no fix. Fewer asks, approvals in advance and fuller answers all end with me approving things I have not read, and a phone screen makes it worse.

So I ask the agents to carry more of it: check a plan against what I have actually said, treat my yes as covering only what was asked, and say in one line what the recommended option leaves out. None of it is measured yet.

When did you last pick an option marked "(Recommended)" without reading the others?
