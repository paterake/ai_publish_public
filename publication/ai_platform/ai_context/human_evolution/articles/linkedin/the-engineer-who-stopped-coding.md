The engineer who stopped coding

The loud story about AI is replacement.

The more interesting story is that it changes where engineering value lives.

Code-writing was never the scarce thing. Judgment was.

If you use AI as a fast typist, the human stays as the quality gate on every output. Speed improves, but the scarce resource (experienced attention) is still consumed at the same rate.

The scalable posture is different:

- encode judgement upstream into durable principles and boundaries
- define what “done” means as a contract, not a feeling
- turn important rules into gates with failure signals

Then the assistant executes inside those constraints, across sessions, without you re-teaching the same rules in every chat.

📌 AI doesn’t make senior engineering obsolete. It makes senior judgement the primary lever.

Question: what is one rule you currently “remember” that should really be a gate?
